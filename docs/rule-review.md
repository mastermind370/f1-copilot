# F-1 Copilot: rules for DSO review

F-1 Copilot computes personal deadlines with fixed rules instead of AI. Each rule below lists
what it computes and the source it relies on. For each one, please mark whether it is correct
**as general guidance** (not for any individual student), and note anything that is wrong,
missing or school-specific. Corrections become new rule versions with your review date.

Every page in the app tells students to confirm with their DSO before acting.

## 2. IRS Form 8843 filing deadline

`form-8843-filing` · version 1 · tax

Nonresident alien F-1 students file Form 8843 for each tax year they were in the US. With US income, it is attached to Form 1040-NR (due mid-April). With no US income, you file Form 8843 alone (due mid-June).

**What the app shows**

- **deadline**: File Form 8843 with Form 1040-NR by
- **deadline**: Mail Form 8843 (no US income) by

**Caveats shown to students**

- Applies to nonresident aliens for tax purposes. Most F-1 students are, for their first five calendar years.
- Weekend dates are moved to Monday. Federal and DC holidays are not modeled. Confirm the IRS date each year.
- This is not tax advice. Your school may offer free tax software for nonresidents.

**Source:** https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-8843

## 3. Grace period after completing your program or OPT

`grace-period-after-completion` · version 1 · status

After you complete your program, or your authorized practical training, you have 60 days to leave the US, transfer to another school, or change status.

**What the app shows**

- **deadline**: Last day of grace period after program completion
- **deadline**: Last day of grace period after OPT ends

**Caveats shown to students**

- A DHS final rule published July 17, 2026 would have cut this grace period to 30 days. A federal court vacated that rule on November 16, 2026, so the 60-day grace period still applies. DHS could appeal. Confirm with your DSO.
- If you withdraw from classes, different (shorter or no) grace periods apply. Talk to your DSO first.

**Source:** https://studyinthestates.dhs.gov/answer/what-do-i-do-if-my-f-1-visa-has-expired

## 4. I-20 travel signature validity

`i20-travel-signature-validity` · version 1 · travel

To re-enter the US, the travel signature on page 2 of your I-20 must be recent: generally within 12 months for enrolled students, and within 6 months on OPT or STEM OPT. Many schools treat it as ending earlier at your program end date or EAD expiration date.

**What the app shows**

- **deadline**: Travel signature valid for re-entry until (enrolled student)
- **deadline**: Travel signature valid for re-entry until (on OPT)

**Caveats shown to students**

- The signature must still be valid on the day you RE-ENTER the US, not the day you leave.
- Some schools use different conventions. Your DSO's guidance takes priority.

**Source:** https://isss.utah.edu/f-1-visa/f-1-students/travel/index.php

## 7. Post-completion OPT filing window

`opt-post-completion-filing-window` · version 1 · opt

USCIS must receive your Form I-765 for post-completion OPT inside this window. It opens 90 days before your program end date and closes 60 days after it. It also closes 30 days after your DSO enters the OPT recommendation in SEVIS, whichever comes first.

**What the app shows**

- **window**: USCIS must receive Form I-765 (post-completion OPT)

**Caveats shown to students**

- The deadline is when USCIS RECEIVES the application, not when you send it.
- Many schools need your OPT request weeks before USCIS's deadline. Check your school's internal deadline.

**Source:** https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/optional-practical-training-opt-for-f-1-students

## 8. OPT unemployment limit

`opt-unemployment-limit` · version 1 · opt

You may not accumulate more than 90 days of unemployment during post-completion OPT. An approved STEM extension adds 60 more, for 150 days in total across both periods.

**What the app shows**

- **limit**: Maximum total unemployment during post-completion OPT
- **limit**: Maximum total unemployment across OPT + STEM extension

**Caveats shown to students**

- Unemployment days are cumulative across the whole OPT period. Your DSO can confirm your count.

**Source:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2

## 9. CPT and OPT eligibility checks

`practical-training-eligibility` · version 1 · eligibility

CPT and OPT generally require one full academic year of lawful full-time enrollment first. Twelve months or more of full-time CPT makes you ineligible for OPT at that degree level.

**What the app shows**

- **notice**: One academic year requirement not yet met
- **notice**: OPT eligibility affected by full-time CPT

**Caveats shown to students**

- SEVP guidance from August 2026 that narrowed which training counts as CPT was stayed by a federal court on November 17, 2026. CPT rules are actively changing. Ask your DSO.
- Some graduate programs that require training in the first year are an exception to the one-year rule.

**Source:** https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-8/chapter-I/subchapter-B/part-214/section-214.2

## 10. STEM OPT extension filing window

`stem-opt-extension-filing-window` · version 1 · opt

USCIS must receive your STEM OPT extension application no earlier than 90 days before your current OPT EAD expires, before it expires, and within 60 days of your DSO's STEM OPT recommendation.

**What the app shows**

- **window**: USCIS must receive STEM OPT extension application

**Caveats shown to students**

- You cannot apply for the STEM extension during the grace period after your OPT ends.
- Requires a STEM-designated degree and an E-Verify employer, plus a Form I-983 training plan.

**Source:** https://www.uscis.gov/working-in-the-united-states/students-and-exchange-visitors/optional-practical-training-extension-for-stem-students-stem-opt
